# GC Land Group — Land Deal Due Diligence System
### Built for the GC Land Group Land Deal Analyzer · FL & AZ Vacant Lot Focus

---

## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

This document is a modular due diligence framework for vacant platted lots in GC Land Group's active FL and AZ target counties. Each section maps to a stage of the deal pipeline:

- **Pre-Offer Filters** → Run before Abner makes any offer (Grace / CRM pre-call scoring)
- **Property Qualification Checklist** → Run before locking up a contract
- **Offer Pricing Safeguards** → Run alongside the Land Deal Analyzer MAO calculation
- **Disposition Readiness Check** → Run before Kyra begins marketing
- **Deal Killer Flags** → Automatic disqualifiers — stop the deal immediately
- **Lessons & Analyst Notes** → Strategic insights for Claude Code build context

---

## SECTION 1 — PRE-OFFER FILTERS
> Run at the lead stage inside the CRM before Abner engages. Data-checkable without a call.

### 1.1 County & Market Fit
- [ ] Parcel is in an active GC Land Group target county
  - FL targets: Lee, Pasco, Polk, Osceola, Marion
  - AZ targets: Mohave, Pinal, Yavapai
- [ ] County has confirmed active comparable sales within the last 18 months
- [ ] County is within the 1–3 hour drive window from a major metro
- [ ] No active development moratorium in the municipality

### 1.2 Parcel Profile
- [ ] Lot size is under 2.0 acres (platted infill profile — not raw acreage)
- [ ] Parcel is classified as vacant land / no improvements on record (county assessor confirmed)
- [ ] Parcel is within a recorded subdivision plat (plat map on file with county)
- [ ] Parcel is NOT in a flood zone (FEMA Zone X preferred; avoid AE, VE, AO)
- [ ] Parcel has a legal lot number and APN confirmed

### 1.3 Seller Profile Signals
- [ ] Owner mailing address is out-of-state (absentee owner)
- [ ] Owner has held parcel 5+ years without a transaction
- [ ] Tax delinquency present (bonus if 2+ years)
- [ ] Owner has NOT listed on MLS, Zillow, or LandWatch recently
- [ ] Owner has multiple parcels in same county (inherited / estate profile)
- [ ] Owner has no recent permit activity on the parcel

**Pre-Offer Score:** Count checkmarks. 10–15 = Priority 1 (same-day Abner contact). 6–9 = Priority 2. Below 6 = Cold.

## SECTION 2 — PROPERTY QUALIFICATION CHECKLIST
> Run before locking up a contract. Verify via county GIS, satellite imagery, and assessor data.

### 2.1 Access & Road Frontage ⚠️ CRITICAL
- [ ] Parcel has direct frontage on a publicly maintained road
  - Confirm road is maintained by county or municipality — not private or unimproved
  - Verify on county GIS road layer — do NOT rely on Google Maps alone
- [ ] If no direct frontage: recorded ingress/egress easement exists in title
- [ ] Parcel is NOT landlocked
- [ ] Physical road access is passable (not seasonal-only, not gated)
- [ ] Driveway or curb cut access is feasible from the road

> **ANALYST NOTE:** Landlocked parcels are the #1 source of wholesaler losses.
> Always verify access on the plat map AND the county road layer.
> A platted road does NOT guarantee a physical road exists.

### 2.2 Utilities
- [ ] Electric service available at or near property line
- [ ] Water confirmed: municipal at road OR well feasibility confirmed
  - FL: Municipal water typical in Lee, Pasco, Polk platted subdivisions
  - AZ: Well or shared water common in Mohave/Yavapai — confirm depth + cost estimates
- [ ] Sewer: municipal at road OR septic feasibility confirmed
  - If prior perc test FAILED: treat as deal killer unless recreational-only buyer
  - FL: Confirm lot is NOT in a septic-restricted zone
- [ ] Distance to nearest utility connection recorded in feet

> **ANALYST NOTE:** A lot with utilities at the road is worth significantly more
> than one requiring $30,000+ in infrastructure. Quantify the utility gap
> and reflect it in your MAO. Buyers will ask immediately.

### 2.3 Zoning & Land Use
- [ ] Zoning confirmed via county GIS or planning department
  - Acceptable: R-1, R-2, R-3, RSF, RS (Residential Single Family variants)
  - Review: A-1, AG (Agricultural) — confirm residential use is permitted
  - Avoid: I (Industrial), C (Commercial) unless specific buyer exists
- [ ] Future Land Use (FLU) map reviewed — residential development permitted
- [ ] No downzoning or rezoning moratorium in progress
- [ ] HOA existence confirmed or denied
  - If HOA exists: confirm active status, dues amount, current payment status
  - CRITICAL: HOA comps must come from WITHIN the same HOA only
  - Confirm no HOA restrictions preventing assignment or quick resale

> **ANALYST NOTE:** HOA status is one of the most common comp errors in platted
> lot deals. Comps outside an HOA are INVALID for a lot inside an HOA.
> HOA lots often sell at a 10–20% discount to non-HOA lots.
> Always separate HOA and non-HOA comps.

### 2.4 Wetlands, Environmentals & Overlays
- [ ] NOT in FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area (check msc.fema.gov)
- [ ] No FEMA-designated wetlands (check fws.gov/wetlands — National Wetlands Inventory)
- [ ] No conservation easement or environmental overlay
  - FL: Check for CARL, Florida Forever, or county conservation restrictions
  - AZ: Check BLM adjacency and scenic/environmental corridors
- [ ] No evidence of environmental contamination (dumping, underground tanks)
- [ ] NOT in a FEMA regulatory floodway
- [ ] No sinkholes mapped on or adjacent to parcel (FL only — check FDEP database)

> **ANALYST NOTE (FL):** Sinkhole risk is real and underpriced by most investors.
> Lee, Polk, and Pasco all have sinkhole-susceptible geology.
> A sinkhole on or adjacent to a parcel blocks retail buyer financing.
> Check FDEP database before ANY FL contract — mandatory, not optional.

### 2.5 Title & Legal Standing
- [ ] Clear chain of title (no gaps, no unknown heirs)
- [ ] No open liens, judgments, or municipal code violations on record
- [ ] Back taxes confirmed: exact amount known and reflected in MAO
- [ ] No IRS tax liens, estate disputes, or probate issues flagged
- [ ] Deed type: Warranty Deed preferred; Quit Claim Deed = additional title research required
- [ ] Property NOT in active bankruptcy proceedings
- [ ] No lis pendens (pending lawsuit) on the parcel
- [ ] Parcel boundaries match legal description

> **ANALYST NOTE:** Quit Claim Deeds are common in delinquent tax / inherited
> scenarios — they do NOT guarantee clear title. If seller offers a QCD,
> always run title before committing to assignment.
> Clean title = faster close and easier disposition.

### 2.6 Buildability & Physical Site
- [ ] Minimum lot size for the zoning district is met
- [ ] Terrain is buildable — not excessively steep, not low-lying (verify via satellite/topo)
  - AZ: Hillside/slope ordinances in Pinal and Yavapai restrict steep lot builds
- [ ] No visible dumping, debris, or junk (check recent satellite imagery)
  - If junk present: disclose fully OR price removal cost into MAO
- [ ] Lot dimensions are standard for the subdivision (not an odd remnant or flag lot)
- [ ] Neighboring parcels are developed or under development (active market signal)
- [ ] No easements crossing the buildable area

## SECTION 3 — OFFER PRICING SAFEGUARDS
> Run alongside the Land Deal Analyzer MAO calculation.

### 3.1 Comparable Sales Methodology
- [ ] Minimum 3 comparable sales pulled (CLOSED sales only — not active listings)
- [ ] Comps are within same subdivision OR within 1.0 mile radius
- [ ] Comps sold within the last 18 months (12 months preferred in active markets)
- [ ] Comps are similar lot size (within 25% of subject lot size)
- [ ] Comps are same zoning classification as subject parcel
- [ ] HOA and non-HOA comps are separated — NEVER mix
- [ ] Price per square foot AND price per acre both calculated
- [ ] Median used (not average) when comp spread is significant

> **ANALYST NOTE:** In platted subdivision lots, price per square foot
> outperforms price per acre as a comp metric. A 0.17-acre lot does NOT
> scale linearly from a 1.0-acre comp. Build both calculations into the
> analyzer output and display them side by side.

### 3.2 MAO Formula

```
MAO = (Median Comp Value x Target Acquisition %) - Estimated Costs

Target Acquisition %:
  Hot lead, clean title, utilities present:     35-45% of median comp
  Warm lead, minor issues:                      30-40% of median comp
  Cold lead or known defects:                   25-35% of median comp

Estimated Costs to subtract:
  Back taxes owed (exact from county)
  Title / closing costs (~$500–$1,500 for vacant land)
  Removal / remediation (junk, debris)
  Assignment fee target ($3,000–$15,000 for infill lots)
```

### 3.3 Three-Offer Output Structure

```
Option A — Fast Cash Close
  Price:       MAO as calculated
  Close:       14–21 days
  Terms:       Cash, no contingencies

Option B — Standard Close
  Price:       MAO + 10–15%
  Close:       30–45 days
  Terms:       Cash with standard due diligence period

Option C — Extended / Seller Finance
  Price:       MAO + 20–30%
  Close:       60–90 days or installment
  Terms:       Down payment + monthly payments at agreed interest rate
```

> **ANALYST NOTE (Apke Brothers):** Never enter a deal where your worst-case
> floor is unclear. "Best case $20K, worst case maybe $9K" is not a deal —
> it is a hope. Your floor must be certain before your ceiling matters.

## SECTION 4 — DISPOSITION READINESS CHECK
> Run before Kyra begins marketing.

### 4.1 Property Package
- [ ] Parcel map / plat map screenshot prepared (lot highlighted)
- [ ] GPS coordinates confirmed and tested in Google Maps
- [ ] Google Maps link generated
- [ ] Satellite imagery screenshot pulled (within 2 years)
- [ ] Lot dimensions confirmed (frontage x depth)
- [ ] Utilities status documented: what is at the road, what is needed
- [ ] Access status documented: road type, maintenance, frontage confirmed
- [ ] Zoning and permitted uses documented
- [ ] HOA status and dues documented (if applicable)
- [ ] Back taxes documented
- [ ] 3 comparable sales documented with price, date, and distance from subject

### 4.2 Listing Requirements
- [ ] Deal one-pager completed (Canva template)
- [ ] Listed on: LandWatch, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist (minimum)
- [ ] Adjacent landowner outreach completed
- [ ] Primary buyer list notified (segmented by county + lot size preference)
- [ ] Ceiling price set; 21-day price reduction trigger pre-scheduled

## SECTION 5 — DEAL KILLER FLAGS
> Any single item = stop the deal or renegotiate before proceeding.

| # | Flag | Action |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | Parcel is landlocked with no recorded easement | STOP — do not contract |
| 2 | FEMA Flood Zone AE, VE, or AO | STOP — near-unsellable in FL |
| 3 | Failed perc test on record | STOP unless recreational-only buyer confirmed |
| 4 | Active HOA with delinquent dues + unknown standing | PAUSE — resolve first |
| 5 | Wetlands covering more than 30% of parcel | STOP — buildable area eliminated |
| 6 | Open IRS lien or active probate dispute | STOP — title cannot be cleared |
| 7 | Price above 60% of median comp after all costs | STOP — margin insufficient |
| 8 | No comparable sales within 18 months within 1 mile | STOP — cannot price |
| 9 | Seller local, emotionally attached, firm at market value | STOP — not motivated |
| 10 | Active MLS listing already exists on the property | STOP — no wholesale advantage |
| 11 | FL sinkhole on or adjacent to parcel | STOP — buyer financing blocked |
| 12 | Conservation easement or environmental overlay | STOP — development rights may be gone |
| 13 | Quit Claim Deed with unknown chain of title | PAUSE — run title search first |

## SECTION 6 — LESSONS & ANALYST NOTES FOR DEAL ANALYZER BUILD
> Implementation notes for Claude Code. Sources: Apke Brothers, Mark Podolsky
> (The Land Geek / Nite Cap), Land Investing Business Secrets Podcast.

---

### 6.1 Never Price Infill Lots Per Acre

**Lesson:** A 0.17-acre lot does not scale linearly from a 1.0-acre comp.
In platted subdivisions, price per square foot or flat comparable value is correct.

**Build note:** Trigger a warning if MAO is calculated using price-per-acre on
any parcel under 0.5 acres. Auto-suggest price-per-square-foot calculation instead.

---

### 6.2 Speed Protects the Deal

**Lesson (Apke Brothers):** Every day of delay gives the seller more time to back out.
Fast operators get under contract in 14–17 days from first contact.
The full process from listing to payout averages 6–7 weeks.

**Build note:** Add a deal timeline tracker. Flag any deal stalled in a single stage
for more than 14 days. Alert JC / Matt via Slack. Display elapsed days prominently
on each deal card in the dashboard.

---

### 6.3 HOA Comp Contamination

**Lesson (Apke Brothers):** Comps outside an HOA are completely invalid for a lot
inside an HOA. Using a non-HOA sold comp to price an HOA lot produces an inflated
MAO that kills the assignment margin.

**Build note:** Add an HOA toggle. When ON: filter comps to HOA-only sales and apply
a suggested 10–20% HOA discount factor to the median comp value. Flag any comp input
where HOA status is unknown or unconfirmed.

---

### 6.4 Three-Offer Output Module

**Lesson (Land Investing Business Secrets — Michael Robertson):** Presenting three
offer options causes sellers to negotiate against themselves. This increases conversion
on warm leads and removes the binary yes/no dynamic of a single offer.

**Build note:** When Abner inputs the MAO, auto-generate the three-offer structure
from Section 3.3 above. Display as a formatted output Abner can reference on a
live seller call without doing mental math.

---

### 6.5 Florida Wetlands and Sinkhole Are Mandatory Inputs

**Lesson:** Florida's NWI data is inconsistent with county GIS. A parcel that
appears buildable may carry a wetlands delineation eliminating most usable area.
Sinkhole risk is underpriced in Lee, Polk, and Pasco counties.

**Build note:** For FL deals, make wetlands check (NWI) and sinkhole check (FDEP)
REQUIRED inputs. Do not display the MAO output until both are confirmed clean.
Show a blocking warning if either check is skipped.

---

### 6.6 Comp Radius Confidence Score

**Lesson:** In high-density platted subdivisions, comps within 0.25 miles are
accurate. In lower-density areas, expanding to 1.0 mile introduces noise —
access, utilities, and development stage vary significantly within a mile.

**Build note:** Build a comp radius selector (0.25mi / 0.5mi / 1.0mi) with a
confidence score (High / Medium / Low) that decreases as radius increases.
Show the number of available comps at each radius level.

---

### 6.7 Disposition Pricing: Three Numbers Required

**Lesson (Apke Brothers):** Overpricing by 10–15% dramatically reduces buyer
response. Set a ceiling, a 21-day reduction trigger, and a floor before listing.

**Build note:** Disposition module outputs three numbers:
  1. Ceiling list price — highest defensible price based on comps
  2. 21-day reduction price — ceiling minus 10–15%
  3. Floor / break-even = min assignment fee + acquisition price + closing costs
Never display a list price below the floor. If ceiling is below floor, flag deal
as a margin failure before Abner contracts it.

---

### 6.8 Platted Lots vs. Rural Acreage Are Different Asset Classes

**Lesson:** Platted lots = more comps, faster velocity, retail buyer pools,
predictable pricing. Rural acreage = higher margins, lower competition, longer
hold times, more complex DD. Do not run the same workflow on both.

**Build note:** Add a property type toggle at the top of the analyzer:
"Platted Infill Lot" vs. "Rural Acreage." Each activates a different set of
DD prompts, comp methodology, MAO formula, and buyer profile assumptions.

---

### 6.9 Document Seller Defect Reasoning

**Lesson (Nite Cap / Apke Brothers):** Most sellers understand there is a defect
with their land and will accept reductions given clear reasoning. Documenting
protects GC Land Group from disputes during assignment or closing.

**Build note:** Add a required negotiation notes field. Before deal status can move
from Negotiating to Under Contract, Abner must document:
  1. Defects disclosed to the seller
  2. Reasoning given for the offer price
  3. Seller's stated response

---

### 6.10 Track Lead Source to Close Rate

**Lesson (Land Investing Business Secrets):** Tracking which lead sources produce
the highest close rates enables smarter list-building and marketing spend decisions.

**Build note:** Add a lead source field on every deal record:
  Options: TaxLead | Land Portal | PropStream | County Assessor Download |
           Cold Call (Grace) | Adjacent Outreach | Referral | Other
Feed into KPI dashboard as a monthly lead source → close rate report.
